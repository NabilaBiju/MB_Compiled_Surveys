Berea College Research Study

Physical Function & Academic Experience Study

Please complete the following survey in one sitting.

Pilot note: This is a pilot version of the survey. One goal is to estimate how long the questionnaires take to complete so the study procedures can be improved before full data collection.

At the end, you will receive brief self-reflection results based on your responses. These results are informational only and are not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or academic evaluation.

Your privacy matters. Your name and B# are collected so your survey can be matched with your academic data. Once it is matched, your name and B# will be redacted and the dataset will be made fully de-identified to maintain your complete privacy.

Consent to Continue
